A review method that schedules each item for review at growing intervals.

## The mechanism

An item reviewed right before it would be forgotten is remembered longer afterwards than one reviewed early. So intervals grow after each successful review (one day, three days, a week, and so on) and shrink after a failure. The decline it works against is the [[forgetting-curve]].

## Why it is efficient

Time goes to the items that are about to slip, not to the ones already known.

## What I said reading it

The scheduling is the whole trick. The cards themselves are ordinary.
