Why wheat dough can hold gas and rise when most other flours cannot.

## Two proteins

When wheat flour is wetted and worked, two proteins, glutenin and gliadin, bond into gluten. Glutenin gives the network elasticity (it springs back); gliadin gives it extensibility (it stretches).

## Kneading and time both build it

Kneading lines the proteins up into sheets. Resting the dough does part of the same work, which is why no-knead breads rely on a long rest instead.

## What I said reading it

The network does not make the gas; it only keeps it.
