A paper flashcard method described by the science journalist Sebastian Leitner in 1972.

## Boxes as a schedule

Cards start in box 1. A card answered correctly moves up one box; a card missed goes back to box 1. Box 1 is reviewed every day, box 2 every few days, and higher boxes less often. No dates are tracked: the box a card sits in is its schedule.

## The same idea on paper

It is [[spaced-repetition]] with no computer: well-known cards drift to rarely reviewed boxes, and the hard ones keep coming back.

## What I said reading it

A schedule can live in where a thing is kept rather than in a date field.
