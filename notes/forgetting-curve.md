Ebbinghaus tested his own memory of lists of nonsense syllables and measured how much relearning time he saved after different delays.

## The shape

The savings fell steeply at first and then levelled off: most of the loss happened within the first hour and the first day, and what remained after a week faded slowly.

## Repetition flattens it

Each relearning made the next decline slower. This is the result that review schedules were later built on.

## What I said reading it

The curve comes from one person learning nonsense syllables. The shape has held up; the exact numbers are his.
